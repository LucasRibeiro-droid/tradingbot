## 1. How TJR describes the whole

- A strategy is "simple concepts, plugging them into an execution order, and then taking the trade" [TJR Day 7, 04:50].
- The concepts are "building blocks" and "execution pieces"; use the ones you understand, and "the more that you know, the better", because each is another confirmation based on orders being filled [TJR Day 14, 07:33–07:55; Day 7, 05:05–06:00].
- Liquidity "is not a strategy, it's a piece that we can plug in to a strategy" [TJR Day 12, 01:41].
- The same approach works on any timeframe and any market, for scalps or swing trades [TJR Day 14, 06:56–07:27; Day 10, 07:42].
- He rejects support and resistance as reasoning ("it bounced off a floor"); every step should be explained by orders being filled [TJR Day 8, 01:19; Day 10, 11:33; Day 14, 01:32].

### The sequence as TJR states it so far

> "Liquidity sweep, break of structure, and we see the trend starting to form. Now I have a
> fair value gap. Oh, I notice it gets filled. Smaller timeframe, break of structure. Boom."
> [TJR Day 14, 06:11–06:21]

> "We got a sweep of liquidity, a break of structure, it hit an order block, reacted, boom,
> that's where I'm entering." [TJR Day 12, 18:11]

> "Literally three simple concepts that we've learned: liquidity sweep, break of structure,
> imbalance fill, break of structure." [TJR Day 18, 07:45–07:51]

> "Liquidity sweep and break of structure, that's the start of the move ... fair value gaps,
> order blocks and equilibrium are used for retracements ... the first option every single
> time for me is going to be an order block." [TJR Day 20, 06:16–07:00]

Assembled, the decision chain taught so far is [Inferred from Days 4, 6, 8–24]:

1. **Context (higher timeframe):** which way price wants to go. Trend [Day 4], and where liquidity rests [Days 10, 12].
2. **Reversal or start of a move:** liquidity is taken (a sweep) [Days 8–12].
3. **Confirmation:** a break of structure the other way, which shows orders were filled [Day 6; Day 8, 07:04].
4. **Wait for the retracement** into a retracement area, especially when the move left no good reward-to-risk: the "patient entry" [Day 14, 03:52; Day 12, 10:54–11:20; Day 18, 05:14–05:46; Day 22, 25:11–25:29]. TJR's order of preference: the **order block** (the leg that swept the liquidity), then a **fair value gap**, then **equilibrium** [Day 20, 06:57–07:49]. Order block "off the first retracement", gaps for "any retracement" [Day 24, 16:49]. A higher-timeframe zone alone is "not enough": it needs the lower-timeframe break [Day 24, 21:00].
5. **Entry trigger:** a break of structure in the trade's direction on a **lower** timeframe, inside that zone; scale down more than once for a precise entry (15m → 5m → 1m) [Day 14, 04:28; Day 18, 03:36–04:33].
6. **Stop** beyond the retracement's high or low [Day 18, 07:12 example], or beyond the whole order block "to play safe"; its size follows the timeframe being played [Day 22, 09:15–09:59, 23:21]. **Targets:** previous liquidity, accumulation areas, gap levels; several take profits, rest to break even [Day 12, 05:56–10:22; Day 18 examples].
7. **No confirmation, no trade** at any step [Day 8, 08:38; Day 12, 07:20]. **No trades before the market opens** [Day 18, 03:13].

Still to be taught: the trading plan (29), putting everything together (30, 32, 33), execution (30, 32, 33), daily bias (34–36),
stop losses (38), take profits (37), position size (39), the trading plan (29).

## 2. Core concepts

### Candles [Day 2]
- A candle is one span of time; the timeframe is how long it takes to close [TJR Day 2, 03:05].
- Bullish: opens at the bottom of the body, closes at the top; bearish the reverse. Wicks show where price went but didn't close [TJR Day 2, 04:25–06:42].
- **Only closed candles count**: "we can only make bias and decision on candles that have formed" [TJR Day 2, 16:50].
- Only single-candle patterns are useful (the row of dojis, hammer, hanging man, inverted hammer); patterns of three or more candles, mountain tops, W's and head-and-shoulders are rejected [TJR Day 2, 07:31–09:52].
- Reads: a strong (full-body) close [TJR Day 2, 15:01]; a long wick rejected with a close at the other end [TJR Day 2, 16:00–18:00]; a close that couldn't hold its extreme, "probably taking liquidity" [TJR Day 2, 09:52–10:40]; dojis show indecision or fading momentum [TJR Day 2, 10:40–13:22].
- A candle read is **one confluence, never a trade on its own**, and never overrides the trend [TJR Day 2, 13:22–15:01].

### Trend [Day 4, refined by Day 6]
- Uptrend: higher highs and higher lows; downtrend: lower highs and lower lows [TJR Day 4, 02:13].
- Markets move on momentum; once trending, odds are it continues [TJR Day 4, 01:01].
- Three states: up, down, consolidation [TJR Day 4, 12:32].

### Highs, lows and break of structure [Day 6]
- A high is a move up then a move down: one up candle then one down candle; the level is the higher wick of the two. A low is the reverse [TJR Day 6, 01:56–05:02].
- **Break of structure (BOS):** a candle **body closes** past the most recent high (up) or low (down). Wicks don't count [TJR Day 6, 07:37–13:25].
- Only the most recent high and low matter [TJR Day 6, 08:54, 18:00–19:00].
- Checklist: identify the trend; in an uptrend watch the lows; in a downtrend watch the highs; a close below the most recent low breaks down; a close above the most recent high breaks up [TJR Day 6, 14:02–18:00, typed on screen].

### Liquidity [Days 8, 10, 12] (details: `liquidity.md`)
- Resting orders (stops, limits) at a price [TJR Day 8, 02:20].
- Banks need it to fill large orders, so price is pushed into it; once filled, price turns [TJR Day 8, 03:52–06:31].
- Above highs: breakout buy stops and shorts' stop losses. Below lows: breakdown sell stops and longs' stop losses [TJR Day 10, 02:49–07:14].
- Prominent highs and lows hold the most: the start of a big trend, double tops, highs or lows that caused a break of structure [TJR Day 12, 03:36–05:56].
- Liquidity is a magnet: price is drawn to it [TJR Day 12, 05:56].

### Fair value gaps [Days 14, 16; incomplete until Day 18] (details: `fair_value_gaps.md`)
- **Fair value gap = liquidity void = imbalance**; the names mean the same thing; TJR prefers "liquidity void" [TJR Day 14, 02:02–02:08].
- It's the opposite of liquidity: a price range where there are **no opposing orders**. A big move up happens because there are no sell orders in that range; a big drop because there are no resting buy orders [TJR Day 14, 02:22–03:04].
- Usually seen as a big candle that moves "with no hesitation" [TJR Day 14, 02:46, 05:31–05:50].
- **Identification:** three candles; bullish = top of candle 1's wick to bottom of candle 3's wick; bearish = bottom of candle 1's wick to top of candle 3's wick; overlapping wicks = not a gap [TJR Day 16, 04:12–05:45, 09:41–11:13].
- **For retracements and continuations, never reversals:** in an uptrend use bullish gaps only [TJR Day 16, 03:22–04:05].
- A gap price pushes past is no longer of interest [TJR Day 18, 09:43]. Refine a gap on a lower timeframe when a lower-timeframe wick already fills part of it [TJR Day 18, 12:03].
- "More of a confirmation rather than an entry" [TJR Day 18, 16:09].

### Equilibrium [Days 26, 28; complete] (details: `equilibrium.md`)
- The 50% of a swing high to swing low (or low to high). Below it is a **discount** (buy, in an uptrend); above it is a **premium** (sell, in a downtrend) [TJR Day 26, 06:32–08:20, 11:16].
- A gauge, not an entry: inside the discount, find an order block, a fair value gap or a break of structure [TJR Day 26, 08:21–08:48]. A fair value gap inside the discount is "the perfect" combination [TJR Day 26, 09:49–10:10].
- The sweep is the "inducement"; the break of structure is "our first primary entry" [TJR Day 26, 09:08–09:16].
- Measure low → high in an uptrend (high → low in a downtrend), dragging it to each new extreme; Gann box or Fibonacci at 0 / 0.5 / 1 [TJR Day 28, 03:57, 08:13–09:16, 18:23]. "Discount" = a good price for the trade's direction [Inferred from Day 28, 02:06 and Day 26, 07:06].
- Equilibrium + a break alone is "not much confirmation"; combine with a gap or block [TJR Day 28, 07:20, 03:30].

### Order blocks [Days 20, 22, 24; complete] (details: `order_blocks.md`)
- **Order block = the move that caused the liquidity sweep**, before the break of structure that shifts the market: the leg up that took the highs before a break down, or the leg down that took the lows before a break up [TJR Day 20, 03:10; Day 22, 02:36–02:46].
- That range is where orders were filled; price tends to retrace into it so more can be filled, then continues the new way [TJR Day 20, 04:37–06:09].
- It's **the whole move**, often several candles; a higher timeframe may show it as one candle; scale down for precision [TJR Day 22, 07:03–08:15].
- **One per trend**, formed at the shift; exists on every timeframe [TJR Day 20, 07:53–09:23].
- Same thing as an **accumulation area** [TJR Day 20, 11:08–11:18].
- Best with a liquidity sweep ("almost always best") [TJR Day 24, 09:52].
- **Boxing:** the whole leg ("your best bet", for beginners) or, as TJR does, "from the wick down to the start of the body", where price often reacts [TJR Day 24, 10:15–11:49, 25:16–25:54].
- Also used as a **take profit** [TJR Day 24, 13:39].

## 3. Market analysis

- **Higher timeframes hold higher power.** Read direction from the higher timeframe; use the lower timeframe only to time the entry [TJR Day 4, 07:41–10:53].
- When they disagree, wait for the lower timeframe to turn in the higher timeframe's direction [TJR Day 4, 08:44].
- For intraday trading, at minimum know where price wants to go for the day [TJR Day 4, 17:23].
- A line chart (closes only) makes direction easy to see [TJR Day 4, 18:00–20:34].
- A break of structure against the higher timeframe is often "just a retracement" [TJR Day 6, 22:13–23:18].
- Liquidity shows where price is likely heading next [TJR Day 12, 05:56]; fair value gaps show where a retracement will likely go [TJR Day 14, 03:16–03:32, 03:52].
- Forecast routine (homework): where price goes this week, the next days, today [TJR Day 4, 23:39–27:04].
- A lower-timeframe break against the higher timeframes signals a retracement, not a trend change [TJR Day 18, 06:36–07:02; Day 6, 22:13].
- **Market open** matters; trades are taken only from the open on; its full value is taught later [TJR Day 18, 02:32–03:19]. Day 22's examples wait for the New York open (09:30) or the London open (15-minute GBPUSD) [TJR Day 22, 12:50, 16:17].
- **News days** (PPI, FOMC): TJR says not to trade them [TJR Day 20, 11:41–12:45]; how to read news is Day 19 (not received).
- **Understand the timeframe you're playing:** it sets the size of the order block, the stop and the targets [TJR Day 22, 09:15–10:39].
- Focus on one or two instruments [TJR Day 4, 27:04].
- Trade in line with the **daily and weekly bias**; a daily bias shift is a daily break of structure [TJR Day 24, 20:23–20:36; taught on Days 34–36].

## 4. Trading conditions

- **Trend vs consolidation:** in consolidation highs and lows form but nothing breaks; "a terrible trading week" [TJR Day 6, 19:42–20:20; Day 4, 12:32].
- **Liquidity taken vs not:** without a sweep the market is "chippy choppy" and can't change direction [TJR Day 12, 03:09].
- **Retracement in a trend:** where fair value gaps come in [TJR Day 14, 03:16–03:52].
- Different situations use different pieces [Inferred from Day 14, 03:52–04:22]:
  - **Reversal** (start of a new trend): liquidity sweep → break of structure the other way.
  - **Continuation** (inside a trend): retracement into an order block or fair value gap → lower-timeframe break of structure in the trend's direction [Day 20, 06:24–07:49; Day 22 examples].

- Liquidity sweep + break of structure [TJR Day 12, 10:02, 11:01 "those are pretty standard"; Day 14, 06:46].
- Liquidity sweep + break of structure + an imbalance filled and reacted off [TJR Day 12, 18:11].
- Liquidity sweep + break of structure + an order block hit and reacted off [TJR Day 12, 18:11].
- Higher-timeframe fair value gap filled + lower-timeframe break of structure in the trend's direction [TJR Day 14, 04:28–05:05, 06:34; Day 16, 12:01].
- A gap in the trend's direction, filled, then a reaction candle (closing in the trend's direction) or a lower-timeframe break of structure [TJR Day 16, 07:58–08:41].
- Liquidity sweep + break of structure + gap fill + lower-timeframe break of structure [TJR Day 18, 07:45]; a gap lined up with liquidity adds confluence [TJR Day 18, 09:53].

## 6. Entry and exit rules (as taught so far)

- **Entry:** after confirmation, not when price first reaches a level [TJR Day 8, 08:38; Day 12, 06:21]. Entry location is the retracement into a fair value gap or order block, triggered by a lower-timeframe break of structure [TJR Day 12, 10:54–11:20; Day 14, 04:28]. Details come in the execution lessons.
- Fair value gaps are "for retracements", "not for entries necessarily", or "for a secondary entry"; liquidity sweeps tell where the market is going [TJR Day 14, 03:52–04:22].
- **Never enter just because price reached a gap;** wait for a reaction or a lower-timeframe break [TJR Day 16, 07:58–08:41].
- Homework example only (not a rule): enter on a bearish close inside a bearish gap after a break down, stop above the previous high, target the previous lows [TJR Day 16, 09:13–09:28].
- **Target:** the liquidity on the other side [TJR Day 12, 08:20–10:22]. Fair value gaps are also places to take profit [TJR Day 12, 13:55], including a gap's start, 50% or end [TJR Day 18, 10:32]; accumulation areas too [TJR Day 18, 07:22, 10:26]. Taking profits is taught on Day 37.
- **Position management shown (examples):** several take profits, the rest stopped at break even [TJR Day 18, 04:33–04:48].
- **Reward-to-risk** matters: waiting for a retracement is how TJR gets a better one [TJR Day 18, 05:28]. No minimum stated yet.
- **Stop loss:** taught on Day 38. Implied: beyond the point where the idea is wrong [Inferred from Day 10, 03:36]. Day 16's example used "above the previous high". Day 22: a stop must fit the timeframe being played (a 4-hour order block needs the stop beyond the whole block, not a tight 1-hour stop); "above the order block itself if you want to play safe" [TJR Day 22, 09:15–09:59, 23:21].
- **Patience:** "if I'm going to get filled all the way up here, why wouldn't I wait for a potentially way better risk reward ratio" [TJR Day 22, 25:11–25:29]. Not every retracement area gets reached: "you just have to be okay with" missing it [TJR Day 22, 19:10].
- **Invalidation:** no reaction or no break of structure after liquidity is reached means no trade [TJR Day 12, 07:20].

## 8. Trading psychology

- Students trade the same strategy as TJR but lose by skipping trades they aren't confident in and bailing out early: execution is the gap [TJR Day 3, 03:25].
- Discipline outlasts motivation; build one uncomfortable habit (21 days to form, 60 to become routine) [TJR Day 3, 07:05–11:37].
- Always seek improvement; 1% better a day [TJR Day 3, 14:27, 26:14].
- Not law: adapt the pieces to what works for you; compete only with yourself [TJR Day 7, 06:00–09:35].
- Confidence in life carries into confidence placing trades [TJR Day 7, 13:20–14:13].
- Balance work with rest [TJR Day 14, 09:18].

## 10. Exceptions and limitations

- Wicks past a level are not a break of structure; they're often liquidity sweeps that fool traders [TJR Day 6, 12:44, 23:58].
- A break against the higher timeframe can be just a retracement [TJR Day 6, 22:31].
- "Not every single high and low will be used as liquidity to fill orders" [TJR Day 12, 19:25].
- "Even when we know for certain, we don't actually know" [TJR Day 6, 24:36].
- Don't copy TJR's markets: his London-open pattern was found by watching GBPJPY [TJR Day 7, 06:55].
- Fair value gaps are not used for reversals [TJR Day 16, 03:22]; they're everywhere and many fail, so they need confirmation [TJR Day 16, 07:26].
- Not every order block gets reached [TJR Day 22, 19:04–19:16].
- Don't trade news days (PPI, FOMC) [TJR Day 20, 11:41].
- [Inferred] Markets that close overnight (stocks like SPY) produce gaps between sessions that TJR's nearly-24-hour markets rarely show; the lessons don't address them.

## 14. [Bot] choices currently in the code

These are not TJR's and are tested, not trusted (`config.py`, `journal/hypotheses.md`):
- Candle thresholds (doji body ≤10% of range, long wick ≥60%, strong close body ≥70%, …).
- Consolidation = price covered <30% of its 20-candle range.
- A prominent high/low = higher/lower than 5 candles on each side (`pool_strength`), standing in for TJR's visual judgment.
- A sweep counts as "just before" a break if within 10 candles.
- Order block: the leg starts at the most recent two-candle low (high) before the sweep's extreme; the range is wick to wick; the sweep must take a `liquidity.py` pool; "broken" = a close beyond the sweep's extreme.
- Fair value gap "entered" = touched; "reacted" = a candle in the zone closing in the gap's direction; "broken" = a close beyond the far side.
- In the test harness (`decision.py`): reversal path entry at the break's close, stop beyond the last 10 candles' extreme, positions closed after 50 candles; continuation path entry at the lower-timeframe break's close, "pushed past" = a close beyond the far side, one target and no partials.
- Instruments SPY and GLD as stand-ins for TJR's S&P 500 and gold.
