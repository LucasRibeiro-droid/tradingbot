## What it is [TJR Day 26]
- The **50% level** of a swing: from a swing high to a swing low, or a swing low to a swing high [06:32–06:50, 07:50–07:57].
- **Discount:** below 50%. That's where banks want to buy in an uptrend [07:55–08:20].
- **Premium:** above 50%. That's where they want to sell in a downtrend [06:50–07:24; 11:16–11:22].
- Why: big money wants a cheaper price to buy and a higher price to sell, like buying chips on sale [05:52–06:24].

## What it's for [TJR Day 26]
- The third **retracement tool**, after order blocks and fair value gaps [09:05–10:12; Day 20, 07:44].
- **A gauge, not an entry:** it shows where people would like to buy or sell. Then find a reason to get in: an order block, a fair value gap, or "even just a break of structure while in a discount" [08:21–08:48].
- **Best paired with a fair value gap:** a gap inside the discount, then a reaction [09:49–10:10].
