## What it learned
1. **Stops under ~0.15% of price are mostly cost.** The 4 trades below 0.15% lost −6.57R (two of them by 1.8R
   and 2.5R, from slippage of a few cents). The 6 at 0.15% or more made +1.05R. Checked against the H56
   backtest (451 trades): R improves steadily with the stop distance; below 0.1% it is −0.74 to −1.79R per
   trade. **Change made (H58): skip gaps whose stop is under 0.15% of price**, −0.56R → −0.30R per trade in the
   backtest, better in both periods (recent −0.27R, n=109; earlier −0.33R, n=125). Still negative: it removes
   cost, it doesn't create an edge (gross ≈ −0.07R either way).
2. **Stacked gaps share levels** (L12): a newer gap's stop is often an older order's entry (SNDK 16:40/16:45,
   AMD 14:15/14:20, SNDK 18:55/19:00), so one move stops one trade and fills the other into its stop. Lead only;
   to test in the backtest next (one change at a time).
3. **Right direction, stopped by noise:** 3 losers reached 3R after the stop-out (MRVL 13:40, AMD 14:20,
   SNDK 19:00). One day; the backtest's MFE distribution will say whether a wider stop pays.
