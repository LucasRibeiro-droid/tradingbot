# Open hypotheses

Each hypothesis changes **one** variable against the current baseline, gets tested on data
the bot did not use to form it, and is only promoted to the new baseline if it wins by a
margin larger than chance. Rejected hypotheses stay here with their result so they are not
retried.

Baseline: `v2-tjr-exits` (2026-10-21: Day 37's take-profit ladder and Day 38's stop beyond the sweep are the default exits, after H19) (one decision process reading all indicators; see knowledge/decision.md; candle settings unchanged since `v0-day02`; swings follow Day 6).
